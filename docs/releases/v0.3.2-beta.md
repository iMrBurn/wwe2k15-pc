## WWE 2K15 PC 0.3.2 beta

**Entrances and victory scenes now run at 60 fps**, and the keyboard keys can be changed in the launcher. **No game data is included.** The launcher offers this update by itself (ABOUT page): your game folder, mods, settings and saves are kept.

### New
- **60 fps entrances and victory scenes** (like the other WWE PC ports): the game used to drop to 30 fps for them, as on the Xbox 360. It now stays at 60. Animation speed, music and the stage videos stay in sync. Tested with a title match (belt included). If you prefer the original 30 fps, untick GRAPHICS > "Entrances and victory scenes at 60 fps". *If a belt or an accessory flickers during an entrance, tell us which wrestler.*
- **Key mapping screen** (CONTROLS tab, "ASSIGNATION DES TOUCHES"): click an action (sticks, A/B/X/Y, bumpers, triggers, D-pad, Start, Back...), then press the key you want. Conflicts are shown in red. Presets: QWERTY, AZERTY, "Arrows + numpad". RESET restores the defaults. Your keys are kept in `launcher-keybinds.ini`; the in-game F4 > Save to config still works. *Limits: one key per action, no mouse buttons.*

### Fixed
- (Already in 0.3.1) White Superstar Threads attires on integrated GPUs at 720p.

### Reminder
- The native renderer is the default since 0.3.0: untick GRAPHICS > "Rendu natif" to return to the normal renderer.
- You need Title Update 4 (`tu00000004_00000000`). With no controller, the launcher turns the keyboard on (Space = A, Enter = Start).

### Known issues
- Intel: waiting for player confirmation on the native renderer; with the normal renderer the first launch can freeze up to ~20 s (use the native one).
- Create a Moveset can get stuck on a loading screen for some players (#25, being looked at).
- Loading screens take about 10-13 s for the biggest matches (no fix found yet).
- Online features (Xbox Live, Xbox Store) do not work. No native Linux build: use Proton.

Bugs: please open an issue with your GPU, CPU, the scene and `logs\wwe2k15_*.log` (and `crash_*.txt`).
Never share the game files, the Title Update or DLC. Not affiliated with WWE, 2K or Yuke's.
