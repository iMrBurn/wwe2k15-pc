## WWE 2K15 PC 0.2.0 beta: experimental native renderer

Update of the unofficial PC port of WWE 2K15. **No game data is included.** If you already have 0.1.x, the launcher offers this update by itself (ABOUT page): your game folder, mods, settings and saves are kept.

### New: native renderer (experimental), made for weak PCs
Tick **GRAPHICS > "Rendu natif (expérimental)"** in the launcher to start `wwe2k15_native.exe` instead of the normal renderer. It draws the game's graphics directly with Vulkan instead of emulating the Xbox 360 GPU, which makes weak and integrated GPUs much faster: on our AMD Radeon integrated GPU a match goes from 15-40 fps to a steady 60 fps (also on 2 CPU cores). On the first launch it prepares the game's shaders on your PC for a few seconds; later launches reuse them. New resolution choices for it: AUTO, 540p, 720p, 1080p, 1440p, 4K, plus a GPU picker.
Limits: it needs a Vulkan 1.2 GPU and driver (otherwise a message tells you to update the driver or untick the option); **the crowd looks brighter than with the normal renderer** (known issue); it was tested on NVIDIA and one AMD integrated GPU only. If something looks wrong, untick the option to go back to the normal renderer and send us `logs\wwe2k15_*.log`.

### Also new
- **Type with your keyboard** in the game's text screens (superstar names, abbreviated names...), like in WWE '12.
- Launcher: clear messages when the game cannot start because the Title Update 4 is missing or does not match your copy.
- AMD cards use the lighter rendering mode automatically (0.1.4), pipeline cache saved between launches.
- The project is now GPL-3.0 (code reused from WWE '12 PC, re:Blue and plume). Third-party licenses are in the `licenses` folder.

### Known issues
- Native renderer: brighter crowd (see above); not tested on desktop AMD/Intel cards yet.
- Integrated GPUs with the normal renderer stay GPU-bound: use the native renderer or the PERFORMANCE preset.
- Online features (Xbox Live, Xbox Store) do not work. No native Linux build: use Proton.

Bugs: please open an issue with your GPU, CPU, the scene and `logs\wwe2k15_*.log`.
Never share the game files, the Title Update or DLC. Not affiliated with WWE, 2K or Yuke's.
