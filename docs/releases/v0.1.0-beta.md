## WWE 2K15 PC 0.1.0 beta

First public beta of the unofficial PC port of WWE 2K15 (Xbox 360), statically recompiled with ReXGlue.
**No game data is included**: you need your own copy of the Xbox 360 game and its Title Update 4.

### What works
- The whole game with Title Update 4 and DLC packages (added from the launcher), 60 fps in matches.
- Vulkan rendering, with the "FSI" path that fixes the black screens during entrances (NVIDIA, Intel).
- Launcher in English, French, German, Spanish and Italian (it also sets the game's language).
- Graphics: 720p, 1080p, 1440p or 4K, presets, texture filtering, FXAA, full screen or window, monitor choice.
- Keyboard (QWERTY or AZERTY) or SDL controllers (Xbox, PlayStation including DualSense).
- Saves: automatic backup before each launch (last 10 kept), manual backup and restore.
- Mods folder: files placed in `mods\` replace the game's own at the same path.
- In-game FPS counter (option).
- Crash and freeze reports in `logs\`, with a notice in the launcher.
- Automatic updates: the launcher checks this page for new versions.
- About half the CPU use of the first builds at the same frame rate; runs on 64-bit CPUs from 2009 or newer (no AVX2 needed).
- The game's "Xbox 360 console" messages say "PC".

### Known issues
- Tested on one PC (NVIDIA GPU). AMD and Intel GPUs, older CPUs and controllers still need testers.
- AMD GPUs run without FSI: black screens are possible during entrances.
- Online features (Xbox Live, Xbox Store) do not work.
- Some heavy scenes can drop below 60 fps on processors with few cores.
- No native Linux build yet: on Linux and Steam Deck, add the launcher to Steam as a non-Steam game and force Proton. Reports welcome.

### Install
1. Unzip anywhere (not in Program Files).
2. Copy your game files (default.xex, pac, sound, movies...) into `game\`, or pick their folder in SETTINGS.
3. SETTINGS > ADD TU / DLC: pick Title Update 4 (`tu00000004_00000000`) and your DLC.
4. Click PLAY.

Bugs: please open an issue with your GPU, the scene, `logs\wwe2k15_*.log` and the crash/freeze report if there is one.
Never share the game files, the Title Update or DLC. Not affiliated with WWE, 2K or Yuke's.
