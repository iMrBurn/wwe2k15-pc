## WWE 2K15 PC 0.2.4 beta

Fixes the **native renderer crash on Intel graphics** and (very likely) the **dark characters in pins and grapples**. **No game data is included.** The launcher offers this update by itself (ABOUT page): your game folder, mods, settings and saves are kept.

### Fixed
- **Native renderer crashed on Intel GPUs** (UHD 620, Iris Xe...) when a match started (#8, #13): a two-channel render target was not recognised as renderable, and Intel's driver crashed when the game cleared it. Now it is created properly (when your GPU supports it) and the unsafe clear is skipped. NVIDIA and AMD were not affected by the crash.
- **Characters too dark in pins and grapples** (native renderer, #14, #16): the close-up shadow target used by those cameras was never drawn; it is now. **Please check and tell us.**
- Unknown graphics formats are mapped instead of being reported as errors.

### Please test
- Intel UHD / Iris Xe players: tick GRAPHICS > "Rendu natif (expérimental)" and start a match; tell us if it works.
- Pins/grapples brightness (#14, #16).
- White models on AMD (#10, #12, #15): the 0.2.3 log shows nothing unresolved now. If you have an AMD driver from 2022 (Vulkan 1.3.2xx), **update your AMD graphics driver** and tell us.

### Reminder
- Weak PC or integrated graphics: tick GRAPHICS > "Rendu natif (expérimental)".
- You need Title Update 4 (`tu00000004_00000000`). Other title updates make the game close at start.
- With no controller detected, the launcher turns the keyboard on for that game (Space = A, Enter = Start).

### Known issues
- Native renderer: only tested on NVIDIA and AMD at the time of writing; Intel reports are very welcome.
- Intel GPUs with the normal renderer: a freeze of up to ~20 s the first time (the driver compiles shaders); use the native renderer.
- Online features (Xbox Live, Xbox Store) do not work. No native Linux build: use Proton.

Bugs: please open an issue with your GPU, CPU, the scene and `logs\wwe2k15_*.log` (and `crash_*.txt`).
Never share the game files, the Title Update or DLC. Not affiliated with WWE, 2K or Yuke's.
