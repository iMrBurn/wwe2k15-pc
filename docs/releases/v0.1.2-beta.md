## WWE 2K15 PC 0.1.2 beta

Update of the unofficial PC port of WWE 2K15. **No game data is included.** If you already have 0.1.x, the launcher offers this update by itself (ABOUT page): your game folder, mods, settings and saves are kept.

### New
- **Play from a disc image (.iso)**: SETTINGS > CHOOSE AN ISO, or put the .iso in the `game` folder. The game reads it directly, nothing to extract. A disc image cannot hold the title update and DLC, so SETTINGS > ADD TU / DLC puts them in `game_update` next to the launcher.

### Known issues
- Tested with an ISO made from an extracted copy of the game; please report how your own ISO behaves.
- Tested on one PC (NVIDIA GPU). AMD and Intel GPUs, older CPUs and controllers still need testers.
- AMD GPUs run without FSI: black screens are possible during entrances.
- Online features (Xbox Live, Xbox Store) do not work.
- No native Linux build yet: on Linux and Steam Deck, add the launcher to Steam as a non-Steam game and force Proton.

Bugs: please open an issue with your GPU, the scene, `logs\wwe2k15_*.log` and the crash/freeze report if there is one.
Never share the game files, the Title Update or DLC. Not affiliated with WWE, 2K or Yuke's.
