## WWE 2K15 PC 0.2.5 beta

Fixes for white wrestler models (native renderer), a hang on PCs with few CPU cores, a crash when no sound device can be opened, and your key bindings are now remembered. **No game data is included.** The launcher offers this update by itself (ABOUT page): your game folder, mods, settings and saves are kept.

### Fixed
- **White / blank wrestler models, native renderer** (#10, #12, #15, #17): the draws that build a wrestler's textures (skin, attire, damage) could be skipped while the game's shaders were still being converted and were never redone. They now wait for their shader (up to 2 s). *Please retest DLC wrestlers, 2K Showcase and Superstar Threads; if it still happens, delete the `cache\native_shaders.bin` file once and try again.* (Idea from Jarrod's WWE 2K14 recomp.)
- **Game freezing for good on PCs with few CPU cores** (2-4 threads): the game woke a helper thread before marking its job as "not done", and on a PC with few cores the late mark could erase the "done" signal. The order is fixed. Could not be reproduced here: **please tell us if your freezes are gone.**
- **Crash right after start when Windows cannot open a sound device:** the game now starts without sound and says so in the log.
- **Key bindings remapped in the game (Fn+F4 > Save to config) are kept** after a restart or an update (#18). They are stored in `launcher-keybinds.ini` next to the launcher.

### New
- **SEPARATE PLAYER** option (CONTROLS tab, with a controller connected and keyboard play on): the keyboard becomes its own player, so two people can play with one controller and the keyboard. *Not tested in a real two-player match yet: feedback welcome.*
- A note before the first native-renderer launch (the game's shaders are prepared, a few seconds).

### Reminder
- Weak PC or integrated graphics: tick GRAPHICS > "Rendu natif (expérimental)".
- You need Title Update 4 (`tu00000004_00000000`). With no controller, the launcher turns the keyboard on (Space = A, Enter = Start).

### Known issues
- White/blank DLC models with the **normal** renderer are not fixed yet (native only in this version).
- Intel: the native renderer crash was fixed in 0.2.4, waiting for confirmation; with the normal renderer the first launch can freeze up to ~20 s.
- Online features (Xbox Live, Xbox Store) do not work. No native Linux build: use Proton.

Bugs: please open an issue with your GPU, CPU, the scene and `logs\wwe2k15_*.log` (and `crash_*.txt`).
Never share the game files, the Title Update or DLC. Not affiliated with WWE, 2K or Yuke's.
