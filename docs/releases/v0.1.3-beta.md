## WWE 2K15 PC 0.1.3 beta

Update of the unofficial PC port of WWE 2K15, focused on smaller PCs. **No game data is included.** If you already have 0.1.x, the launcher offers this update by itself (ABOUT page): your game folder, mods, settings and saves are kept.

### Smaller PCs
- **Lighter graphics option**: GRAPHICS > untick "accurate rendering (FSI)", or pick the PERFORMANCE preset. It needs about 35-40 % less GPU work (measured), recommended for GTX 1650-class cards and below. The first matches may stutter a little while the shader cache fills up.
- **Less CPU work** in the thread that translates the game's graphics commands (about -10 %), which matters most on 2-4 core processors.

### Fixed / improved
- SETTINGS > BROWSE now picks a **file**: `default.xex` (its folder is used) or an `.iso`. Before, it asked for a folder and `default.xex` was not visible.
- Mods now also work when playing from an ISO (they win over the title update files).
- Logs: no more hundreds of false "BaseHeap::Alloc" errors (it was the game probing free memory), and one line telling which rendering path is used (FSI or host).
- Mods guide: `docs/MODS.md` on GitHub.

### Known issues
- Tested on one PC (NVIDIA RTX 5070 Ti, and its AMD integrated GPU). Reports from GTX 1650-class cards, AMD and Intel GPUs, older CPUs and Steam Deck are very welcome (with the log file).
- Online features (Xbox Live, Xbox Store) do not work.
- No native Linux build yet: on Linux and Steam Deck, add the launcher to Steam as a non-Steam game and force Proton.

Bugs: please open an issue with your GPU, CPU, the scene, `logs\wwe2k15_*.log` and the crash/freeze report if there is one.
Never share the game files, the Title Update or DLC. Not affiliated with WWE, 2K or Yuke's.
