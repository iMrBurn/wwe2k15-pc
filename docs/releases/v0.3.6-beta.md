## WWE 2K15 PC 0.3.6 beta

**In-game settings menu (F10), a Quit button, and experimental options for logos, camera cuts and Universe.** **No game data is included.** The launcher offers this update by itself (ABOUT page): your game folder, mods, settings and saves are kept. *Nothing changes for normal play unless you turn the experimental options on.*

### New
- **In-game settings menu**: press **F10** (or hold **Back + Start** on a controller for about half a second; Steam Deck friendly) at any time. Keyboard (arrows / Enter / Esc), controller and mouse work. It lists fullscreen, resolution (360p to 2160p with the native renderer), vsync, anti-aliasing, 60 fps entrances, FPS counter and the experimental options below. Press **Apply and save** to keep your changes (settings marked with a star apply at the next launch); **Cancel**, B or Esc writes nothing. While the menu is open the game does not receive any key or button. A **Quit game** button (with a Yes/No question) closes the game cleanly. The game keeps running behind the window, so open it from a menu or the pause screen.
- **Logo import, keep proportions** (experimental, F10): the picture you import into Create-a-Logo can keep its ratio, centered with transparent margins, instead of being stretched to a square.
- **Camera cuts on finishers and grapples** (experimental, F10): the game excludes finishers and signature moves from camera cuts. These two options let the cuts happen there too. *Not fully tested by eye: tell us what you see, and turn them off if the camera does something odd.*
- **Universe "free shows"** (experimental, F10; use a COPY of your save): lets you save a Major show without a World title or with an empty roster and play it (it simply has no matches), so you can run a Universe with one Major show plus Minor shows. Tested over about 40 simulated days; a tiny roster of 1 or 2 wrestlers is NOT tested.

### Reminder
- 0.3.5: portraits of created superstars and thumbnails of created arenas are saved clean (open and save again the ones created before), and a fix for the freeze in Create a Championship.
- The native renderer is the default since 0.3.0: untick GRAPHICS > "Rendu natif" to return to the normal renderer.
- You need Title Update 4 (`tu00000004_00000000`). With no controller, the launcher turns the keyboard on (Space = A, Enter = Start).

### Known issues
- The "SHOP" entry of the main menu still shows its network error message (it cannot be turned into EXIT): use F10 > Quit game.
- Limit on how much a logo can be stretched in Create an Arena (#36): looked at, not found yet.
- Multi-man matches can stutter on slow CPUs (#27, #28). Loading screens take about 10-13 s for the biggest matches (the game itself waits). Showcase: a crash after a cutscene in a casket match and sound cutting out in videos on a 2-core CPU were reported (#24); a crash in the render thread (#39) is being looked at.
- Online features (Xbox Live, Xbox Store) do not work. No native Linux build: use Proton.

Bugs: please open an issue with your GPU, CPU, the scene and `logs\wwe2k15_*.log` (and `crash_*.txt`).
Never share the game files, the Title Update or DLC. Not affiliated with WWE, 2K or Yuke's.
