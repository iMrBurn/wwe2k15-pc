## WWE 2K15 PC 0.5.0 beta

**Protection against the Showcase / story cutscene crashes, a launcher with a DIAGNOSTIC page and a suggested Steam Deck / small PC profile, RecompLauncher support.** **No game data is included.** The launcher offers this update by itself (ABOUT page): your game folder, mods, settings and saves are kept.

### Fixed
- **Crashes in Showcase / story cutscenes** (#39, #44; the freeze #45 and the crash #24 look related): the game's list of display commands could call a method on an object that no longer exists. The port now skips such a call (logged as "crash guard") and ends a command list that spins forever. *Written from the crash reports and checked to compile and start, but not yet seen working in the exact scenes that crashed: please tell us if "Who Got NXT" (4th match), the Alberto Del Rio cutscene or the casket match still crash.* Turn it off with `wwe2k15_crash_guards = false` in `wwe2k15.toml`.

### New
- **DIAGNOSTIC page in the launcher** (new tab): checks your game folder, Title Update 4, DLC packages, graphics card and Vulkan version, `dxcompiler.dll` and the data folder, and **COPY THE REPORT** gives a text to paste in an issue (your user name is replaced).
- **Steam Deck / small PC profile is now suggested, not silently applied**: a window on the first start lists what would change (native renderer, internal resolution, full screen) with Apply / Not now / Don't ask again, and **CANCEL THE PROFILE** in SETTINGS restores your previous settings. It never lowers a setting that is already lower.
- **Steam Deck detected by its chip name** (VANGOGH, Custom GPU 0405 / 0932) as well as `SteamDeck=1` (desktop mode, other launchers). **Small PC score**: integrated GPU, little video memory (under 3.5 GB), 4 physical cores or fewer, 8 GB of RAM or less; a PC with all four gets 540p automatically (never on a Deck, never when you choose a resolution; `svr_auto_lowend = false` turns it off).
- **"Mute the game" option** in the launcher settings.
- **RecompLauncher**: the zip now contains a `recomplauncher.json` (installs from our releases and starts our launcher) — not tested with RecompLauncher itself yet (#43).
- Advanced: `svr_shader_wait_ms` (command line, default unchanged) sets how long a draw waits for a shader being converted; on a very slow CPU a small value such as 50 may reduce the freezes of the first start, at the risk of a missing object for a moment.

### Reminder
- 0.4.0/0.4.1: accents in paths fixed (#40), EXIT in the main menu, weak PC profile (#41), F10 in-game menu (or hold **Back + Start**), Steam Deck groundwork.
- The native renderer is the default since 0.3.0: untick GRAPHICS > "Rendu natif" to return to the normal renderer. Both use Vulkan 1.2; there is no Direct3D 11/12 mode in 2K15.
- You need Title Update 4 (`tu00000004_00000000`). With no controller, the launcher turns the keyboard on (Space = A, Enter = Start).

### Known issues
- **Create a Championship can still freeze** when you move or resize logos or reliefs on some PCs (#33): we found the cause is in the game's graphics command ring, not in the Showcase crashes, and are working on it.
- A 30 fps mode is not available: our attempt made the game crash after the title screen. Options to switch off shadows or motion blur are not available either.
- Limit on how much a logo can be stretched in Create an Arena (#36): not found yet.
- The first start on a weak PC freezes a few seconds while the game prepares its shaders (about 90 s on 2 cores, once). Multi-man matches can stutter on slow CPUs (#27, #28).
- Online features (Xbox Live, Xbox Store) do not work. No native Linux build: use Proton. Not tested on a real Steam Deck.

Bugs: please open an issue with your GPU, CPU, the scene and `logs\wwe2k15_*.log` (and `crash_*.txt`), or use DIAGNOSTIC > COPY THE REPORT.
Never share the game files, the Title Update or DLC. Not affiliated with WWE, 2K or Yuke's.
