## WWE 2K15 PC 0.1.1 beta

Update of the unofficial PC port of WWE 2K15. **No game data is included.** If you already have 0.1.0, the launcher offers this update by itself (ABOUT page): your game folder, mods, settings and saves are kept.

### Fixed
- **Superstar Threads and created attire**: attire and skin no longer show scrambled colour bands, in the editor, during entrances and in matches, from far and close. The game builds these textures on the GPU and reads them back on the CPU; that readback now happens, only for the textures that need it, so matches stay at 60 fps.
- **Screen tearing**: frames are now presented without tearing. A "Vertical sync" box in GRAPHICS turns it off for the lowest latency.
- A Vulkan crash ("device lost") when the resolve readback was enabled.

### Known issues
- Tested on one PC (NVIDIA GPU). AMD and Intel GPUs, older CPUs and controllers still need testers.
- AMD GPUs run without FSI: black screens are possible during entrances.
- Online features (Xbox Live, Xbox Store) do not work.
- No native Linux build yet: on Linux and Steam Deck, add the launcher to Steam as a non-Steam game and force Proton.

Bugs: please open an issue with your GPU, the scene, `logs\wwe2k15_*.log` and the crash/freeze report if there is one.
Never share the game files, the Title Update or DLC. Not affiliated with WWE, 2K or Yuke's.
