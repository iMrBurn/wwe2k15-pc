## WWE 2K15 PC 0.2.2 beta

Smoother first launches, clearer launcher messages. **No game data is included.** The launcher offers this update by itself (ABOUT page): your game folder, mods, settings and saves are kept.

### Improved
- **Less stutter on the first matches** (normal renderer, especially AMD): the draws without a pixel shader (depth/shadow passes) are now compiled in the background instead of freezing the game. On an AMD integrated GPU with an empty cache, the longest freeze went from 2.2 s to 0.18 s.
- **Launcher, PLAY page:**
  - "No controller detected" note with a button to turn on keyboard play (and a reminder of the keys).
  - "Wrong Title Update" note when the game stops at start because your Title Update is not Title Update 4 (version 0.0.6.4).
  - Clear notes when `default.xex` or the Title Update is missing/empty (0.2.0).
- **GPU choice:** the native renderer logs every Vulkan GPU and falls back to another one if the chosen GPU cannot run the game. The normal renderer picks the lighter mode on older NVIDIA cards (like the GeForce 940MX).
- **README** rewritten as a step-by-step guide with the usual start-up problems.

### Reminder
- Weak PC or integrated graphics: tick GRAPHICS > "Rendu natif (expérimental)".
- You need Title Update 4 (`tu00000004_00000000`). Other title updates make the game close at start.

### Known issues
- Native renderer: tested on NVIDIA and AMD only; Intel reports are welcome. Shadows/post-processing were not compared side by side with the normal renderer yet.
- White/glitched wrestler models on AMD (#10, #12): possibly fixed in 0.2.1, waiting for feedback.
- Online features (Xbox Live, Xbox Store) do not work. No native Linux build: use Proton.

Bugs: please open an issue with your GPU, CPU, the scene and `logs\wwe2k15_*.log`.
Never share the game files, the Title Update or DLC. Not affiliated with WWE, 2K or Yuke's.
