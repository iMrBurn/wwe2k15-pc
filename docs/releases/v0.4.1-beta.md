## WWE 2K15 PC 0.4.1 beta

**A small update for Steam Deck and small PCs: the Deck is now recognised by the name of its graphics chip too, and the automatic resolution knows more small PCs.** **No game data is included.** The launcher offers this update by itself (ABOUT page): your game folder, mods, settings and saves are kept.

### New
- **Steam Deck detection by graphics card name**: besides the `SteamDeck=1` variable (only set in game mode), the launcher and the game now recognise the Deck's chip (VANGOGH, Custom GPU 0405 / 0932). In desktop mode or from another launcher the Deck now gets its own first-start settings too (native renderer, 720p, full screen, vsync, no keyboard mode) and stays at 720p.
- **Small PC score for the automatic resolution**: the game looks at the graphics card it really uses (not another card of a two-GPU laptop): integrated GPU, little video memory (under 3.5 GB), 4 physical cores or fewer, 8 GB of RAM or less. A PC that matches all four now gets 540p automatically (the weak PC rule of 0.4.0 for 4 threads or fewer is unchanged). Never on a Steam Deck, never when you choose a resolution yourself; turn it off with `svr_auto_lowend = false`. The log line "small PC (score ...)" shows when it applies.
- Not tested on a real Steam Deck yet: please tell us how it goes (version, Proton version, `logs\wwe2k15_*.log`).

### Reminder
- 0.4.0: accents in paths fixed (#40), launcher installs in the background, EXIT in the main menu, weak PC profile (#41), F10 in-game menu (or hold **Back + Start**).
- The native renderer is the default since 0.3.0: untick GRAPHICS > "Rendu natif" to return to the normal renderer.
- You need Title Update 4 (`tu00000004_00000000`). With no controller, the launcher turns the keyboard on (Space = A, Enter = Start).

### Known issues
- **Create a Championship can still freeze** when you move or resize logos or reliefs on some PCs (#33): we are working on it.
- Limit on how much a logo can be stretched in Create an Arena (#36): not found yet.
- Multi-man matches can stutter on slow CPUs (#27, #28). The first start on a weak PC freezes a few seconds while the game prepares its shaders (about 90 s on 2 cores, once). A 30 fps mode is not available yet.
- Online features (Xbox Live, Xbox Store) do not work. No native Linux build: use Proton.

Bugs: please open an issue with your GPU, CPU, the scene and `logs\wwe2k15_*.log` (and `crash_*.txt`).
Never share the game files, the Title Update or DLC. Not affiliated with WWE, 2K or Yuke's.
